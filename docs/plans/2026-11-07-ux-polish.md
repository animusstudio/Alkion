# UX polish: body sizes, workload panel, loaders, Explore and History

Date: 2026-11-07. Lane: **Standard**. Status: **Approved** by the owner on 2026-11-07,
with the gesture variant chosen by the owner.
Parent: [development](../alkion/development.md).
Baseline: `c0b6f06`; branch `feat/ux-polish-20261107`.

## Outcome and evidence

The owner reviewed the app on Nothing A024 and asked for a gesture change and
five improvements.

0. **One-finger vertical drag when zoomed.** The owner says the Explore inertia
   feels good, which closes the explore-gestures T6 feel check. When zoomed in,
   though, the owner wants one-finger up/down to pan the model, rather than
   needing two fingers.

1. **Today card body is small.** `BodyFigure` is a fixed 128×260 box
   (`body_figure.dart:98`), and its art is 128×212. It sits beside the info
   column and leaves empty space on the card.
2. **The workload panel is cluttered.**
   - The entry is a bare 12 pt `TextButton`, "Workout workload", which reads as
     plain text.
   - The sheet stacks these sections:
     - a mode line;
     - eight view choices;
     - the art, plus Front, Back and Pause buttons;
     - two meaning paragraphs and a legend;
     - 13 region counts;
     - "Whole-workout coverage", "Documented anatomy", and an
       "Assessment and display details" expander.
   - The owner wants only the close-up body with the legend, and perhaps the
     per-region set numbers.
3. **Loaders look poor.**
   - The body loader is a static 13-line ASCII silhouette at 35% alpha.
   - `CatalogGlyphSkeleton` is a static dot grid.
   - Exercise artwork has no loading state.
   - Nine screens use stock `CircularProgressIndicator` or
     `LinearProgressIndicator`, and Today shows a blank 290 dp box.
   - The owner pointed to [ascii-reveal](https://github.com/quanhoangindex/ascii-reveal)
     as the reference. Its skeleton is a faint glyph pattern in the content's
     exact rectangle with a denser band sweeping across. When data lands, it
     dissolves into the real content.
4. **Explore muscles wastes space.**
   - The canvas is full width but a fixed 300 dp high.
   - The muscle list is a collapsed "Muscle list" disclosure holding a chip
     `Wrap`.
5. **History's three summary cards differ in height.** The `Row` has no stretch
   or `IntrinsicHeight`, and labels wrap to one or two lines by locale. The owner
   asked whether plain separators would fit the design better. They would: the
   design brief says "proximity and dividers group content", and Today's
   `StatTile` row is already flat with rules.

## Decisions

- **Zoomed one-finger drag (owner choice, 2026-11-07):**
  - Above 1× zoom (implemented as above 1.02×), the vertical part of a
    one-finger drag pans the model
    vertically, and the horizontal part still yaws.
  - The release coasts both with the existing time constant and eases at the pan
    bound.
  - Pitch keeps its current value while zoomed.
  - At 1×, vertical drag still tilts, as now.
  - Two-finger pinch and pan, taps, picking, inertia stop and reduced motion are
    unchanged.
  - The mode is chosen when the gesture starts or re-bases, so it never switches
    mid-drag.

- **One glyph loading system** in `lib/widgets/glyph_loading.dart`:
  - `GlyphSkeleton` fills the reserved rectangle with a faint dither glyph grid
    in mono. Its shapes are box, lines and body silhouette (the silhouette was
    later removed by the device feedback amendment).
  - While a real load runs, a denser band sweeps across it on one ticker of
    about 1.4 s.
  - `GlyphReveal` dissolves the skeleton into the content when the load
    finishes. Cells fall through the glyph ramp and clear in a wave from the
    centre within `Motion.slow`, 300 ms (later `Motion.reveal`, 500 ms, by the
    device feedback amendment).
  - Colours come from tokens only. Under reduced motion it draws a static
    pattern and the swap is instant.
  - The sweep runs only while a load is pending; it is not decorative. This
    amends the 2026-11-01 "no infinite glyph scrambling" rule.
  - No new package.
- **Applied everywhere a user waits:**
  - the body artwork loader on Today, the workload sheet and Explore. The native
    view stays hidden under the skeleton until ready, then the overlay
    dissolves away;
  - `CatalogGlyphSkeleton`, `CatalogReadyReveal` and the Today 290 dp
    placeholder;
  - exercise artwork, which gets a loading state it lacks today;
  - every full-screen or card-level spinner listed in the evidence, each as a
    skeleton shaped like the content it waits for.
  - Small inline busy marks inside buttons stay as they are: the 14 dp export row
    and the linear save bars.
- **Today card:**
  - The figure grows to fill the card's height beside the info column, at least
    about 1.35× (about 172×300 art) where width allows.
  - The pager height formula follows.
  - The narrow and large-text fallback still stacks the figure under the info,
    at a larger centred size.
- **Workload entry:** a clear 48 dp tonal button with a body glyph,
  "Workload", and a chevron, used on Today and the session summary. The
  active-session menu row is unchanged.
- **Workload sheet:**
  - The sheet keeps only the following:
    - header, title and close;
    - a one-line mode subtitle: Planned, Completed or Saved;
    - the large close-up body, at least 420 dp high, rotated by drag as in
      Explore;
    - the compact six-bin legend in one row;
    - the region set counts, as a compact two-column list.
  - Removed:
    - the eight view chips;
    - Front, Back and Pause;
    - both meaning paragraphs;
    - whole-workout coverage;
    - documented anatomy;
    - assessment and display details;
    - the recorded/current choice.
  - The view follows the existing automatic selection, so stretch-only work
    shows stretch. Saved workouts use the recorded assessment, which is already
    the default.
  - Reload stays, shown only on error.
  - [character-body-runtime](../alkion/character-body-runtime.md) is amended to
    match. The underlying counts, bins and provenance code stay; only the
    display is removed.
- **Explore muscles:**
  - From 360 dp up, a two-column layout: body canvas on the left (about 55%,
    filling the sheet height above the pinned button) and an always-visible,
    independently scrolling muscle list on the right.
  - Below 360 dp, or at large text, it stacks: a taller canvas (about 55% of
    the sheet) above the inline list.
  - The list becomes rows (glyph, label, selected state), grouped by body area
    where the index provides it; otherwise it stays alphabetical. Unclassified
    is the last row.
  - The disclosure is removed. Selecting a row turns the body to the side where
    that muscle is visible when that data exists.
  - Gestures, zoom and inertia are unchanged.
- **History summary:**
  - The three cards become one flat band of three equal-width sections,
    separated by vertical `line` rules, with no boxes.
  - Every label reserves two lines, so all three sections have the same height.
  - Below 360 dp at large text the band stacks like Today's `StatTile` row.
- Non-goals: no change to counts, bins, the catalog, gestures, the renderer
  look, or the active-session screen layout.

## Acceptance

- A0. Above 1.02× zoom, a one-finger vertical drag pans and does not tilt. At
  1.02× or less it tilts. The small margin above 1× exists because at that zoom
  the pan bound is only about 1% of the canvas height. The coast and bounds behave as for two-finger pan. Recognizer
  tests use real pointer sequences across the 1× boundary, and the existing
  gesture and inertia tests pass.
- A1. Every listed loader uses `GlyphSkeleton` with the same rectangle as its
  content, so there is no layout jump. The sweep runs only while loading. The
  dissolve takes 300 ms or less (amended to 500 ms). Reduced motion is static. Analyze finds no
  `CircularProgressIndicator` left outside inline button marks.
- A2. The Today figure is at least 1.35× its old size at 412 dp and fits at
  320 dp with 2× text. The workload entry is a 48 dp button with semantics.
- A3. The workload sheet shows exactly the header, the mode subtitle, the body,
  the legend and the region counts. Its tests are updated, and the removed
  strings are deleted from the four locales.
- A4. Explore shows two columns at 412 dp and stacks at 320 dp with 2× text.
  The list is visible without a disclosure. Selection syncs both ways. The
  existing gesture, zoom and layout-sweep tests pass, adjusted only for the new
  list.
- A5. The History band has equal section heights in all four locales at
  320/412 dp and 1×/2× text, with no card boxes.
- A6. Analyze, the full suite (except the 3 known `catalog_metric_controls`
  failures), format, and the body JS, material and bridge checks all pass.
  Host screenshots of each changed screen, in both themes, are captured
  privately. The owner confirms on A024.

## Device feedback amendment (owner, 2026-11-07)

The owner's check of the `e0f7afa` build on A024 found the following:

- **Workload panel:** approved as built ("very good").
- **Loaders:** "definitely better", with these changes requested:
  - drop the character silhouette shape;
  - every loader is a plain rectangle with the sweep;
  - the rectangle's edges fade out so it blends into the card;
  - the reveal after loading is a little slower.
- **Startup:** the catalog-shaped startup skeleton should become the Alkion logo
  with a loading animation.
- **Explore:** the body-left, list-right layout is rejected. The owner chose a
  full-width body with a horizontal muscle strip under it.

Decisions:
- **T7 — Loader refinement:**
  - Remove the `.silhouette` shape and `kGlyphBodySilhouette`. The body artwork
    loader becomes a feathered `.box` the size of the canvas.
  - All skeleton shapes get soft edges, fading to transparent over about 12–16 dp
    (or about 25% of a small block). This is done with a mask in the painter,
    not an extra widget layer.
  - The reveal uses a new `Motion.reveal` of 500 ms, replacing `Motion.slow`
    for the dissolve. This amends A1's "300 ms or less".
  - Startup: while the catalog loads, the screen shows the Alkion brand mark
    centred, matching the launch logo so the hand-off from the native splash
    does not jump, with a slim feathered glyph band sweeping under it. When the
    catalog is ready, it dissolves into Home.
  - Reduced motion: everything is static and the swap is instant.
- **T8 — Explore strip:**
  - The canvas spans the sheet width and fills the height between the view
    controls and the bottom area. It is no longer a column.
  - Under it: the selected-muscle label, then one horizontal, snapping strip of
    muscle chips (glyph and name, 48 dp). The selected chip is centred. The
    strip scrolls on its own and the page does not scroll. Unclassified is the
    last chip.
  - Tapping a chip selects it and turns the body as now. A canvas pick centres
    its chip.
  - Short or landscape viewports keep the stacked fallback with page scroll.
  - The renderer's pick readback is reported at 411×911 dp. A frame-time
    measurement on A024 decides whether a resolution cap is needed.
  - The two-column layout and its tests are removed. Gestures, zoom, pan when
    zoomed and inertia are unchanged.
- **T4 tests:** the stacked band gets a height bound, row mode asserts 18 sp,
  and the layout mode is asserted per case.

## No tilt in Explore (owner, 2026-11-07)

The owner disabled tilt in Explore muscles, leaving only zoom, rotate and pan.
This supersedes the T0 rule "at 1×, vertical drag tilts" and the explore-gestures
one-finger pitch.

- **T9:**
  - A one-finger drag always yaws with its horizontal part and pans with its
    vertical part, within the pan bound. At 1× the bound is 0, so the vertical
    part does nothing there. Pitch stays 0.
  - The 1.02× mode choice and pitch inertia go away. The release coasts yaw
    and vertical pan as now.
  - Pinch, two-finger pan, taps and picking, Front/Back/Side/Reset, inertia
    stop and reduced motion are unchanged.
  - Bridge protocol 5 keeps its pitch field, always sent as 0, so the native
    and JS sides do not change.
