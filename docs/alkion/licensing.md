## Optional separate commercial arrangements

Users who comply with AGPL or CC BY-SA may use the covered material commercially
without buying an exception. A separate written agreement may be possible for
particular owner-controlled rights, for example permission to distribute
owner-controlled software under different source-sharing terms, or to use an
owner-controlled illustration under different attribution/ShareAlike terms.

Contact [psychonaut0](https://github.com/psychonaut0), the repository owner,
through the contact route on that profile to discuss the material, intended use
and requested exception. This is an invitation to discuss terms, not a price,
binding sales agreement, automatic exception or promise that an exception can
cover the entire program. The standard licenses remain available independently.
See [GNU guidance on selling exceptions](https://www.gnu.org/philosophy/selling-exceptions.html)
and [CC guidance on separate agreements](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#can-i-enter-into-separate-or-supplemental-agreements-with-users-of-my-work).

Other contributors retain copyright in their contributions. An ordinary AGPL
contribution does not transfer copyright or silently authorize proprietary
relicensing. An exception covering another contributor's work requires the
necessary separate permission; otherwise retain its applicable license, exclude
it or replace it lawfully. The owner cannot waive third-party software, font,
Workout Guide, private-model or other source restrictions merely by offering
commercial terms. No contributor assignment or commercial contract is created
by this guide.
